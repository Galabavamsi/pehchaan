## 1. India: what has RBI (and the courts/MHA) actually put in force vs proposed?

### Takeaway
The APP-specific controls Pehchaan positions against (1-hour lag above ₹10,000, trusted person for 70+, kill switch) are **still only a discussion paper** (9 Apr 2026, comments closed 8 May 2026); I found no draft or final directions on them as of 24 Oct 2026. What **is in force** is broader: risk-based authentication (Authentication Directions 2025, live 1 Apr 2026) and the Digital Payment Security Controls Directions 2026 (31 Jul 2026), which require banks to monitor new-beneficiary additions and payees with recorded vishing fraud. Pressure from the Supreme Court/MHA "digital arrest" case is pushing RBI toward debit-hold SOPs and a liability framework for fraud-induced *authorised* transactions.

### Cited Findings
**RBI Discussion Paper "Exploring safeguards in digital payments to curb frauds" (9 Apr 2026): PROPOSED**
- Option 1, time lag: a mandatory **one-hour lag at the payer's bank for APP transfers at or above ₹10,000**, cancellable by the payer during the hour; applies to individuals, sole proprietors and partnerships; exempts merchant payments, recurring mandates (e-mandate/NACH) and cheques; whitelisting (transaction-specific vs payee-specific) is left as an open question (Q6); bank "may seek reconfirmation" if the transaction looks suspicious. The paper does not specify a separate lag only for new payees. [P] — [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/PublicationsView.aspx?id=23810)
- Option 2, trusted person: **mandatory for customers aged 70+ and persons with disabilities, for APP transfers above ₹50,000**, optional for others; changing the trusted person needs a 24-hour cooling period; opt-out after a 24-hour wait with risks explained. [P] — [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/PublicationsView.aspx?id=23810)
- Option 3, "shadow credit" on low-turnover accounts (₹25 lakh annual credit ceiling; excess held, reversed after ~30 days if not verified). Option 4, customer controls: per-mode on/off, limits, and a one-action **kill switch** that disables all digital payments. [P] — [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/PublicationsView.aspx?id=23810)
- Data cited: transactions above ₹10,000 are ~45% of NCRP fraud cases by volume but ~98.5% by value; ~92% of fraud value is above ₹50,000. The paper gives **no figure on fraud under phone-call pressure** (it names bogus call centres and impersonation as tactics only). [P] — [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/PublicationsView.aspx?id=23810)
- International models it cites: UK hold "up to 72 hours"; Singapore 12-hour cooling-off for high-risk actions plus kill switch; Sweden (May 2024 Swedish Bankers' Association package incl. trusted-person approval); Australian "digital padlock"; US/Ireland trusted contacts. [P] — [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/PublicationsView.aspx?id=23810). Note: the UK statute actually allows **up to four business days**, not 72 hours (see Section 5) — [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.co.uk/insights/final-guidance-on-delayed-payments-to-prevent-app-fraud/), [Bird & Bird](https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2024/uk/new-rules-for-reimbursement-on-authorised-push-payment-fraud-app-are-coming-into-force-in-the-uk).
- Status: comments closed **8 May 2026**; "RBI will consider issuing draft guidelines". [P] — [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/PublicationsView.aspx?id=23810); press framed it as a proposal ("RBI paper suggests 1-hour lag") — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/rbi-proposes-1-hour-delay-for-digital-payments-above-10-000-to-curb-fraud-126040901368_1.html), [Mint](https://www.livemint.com/money/rbi-moots-safeguards-to-curb-digital-payment-fraud-how-will-they-protect-bank-customers-11778077924693.html). Some press reports describe the lag as applying to new payees; the paper itself does not. [S]

**RBI (Authentication Mechanisms for Digital Payment Transactions) Directions, 2025: IN FORCE from 1 Apr 2026**
- Issued 25 Sep 2025 (RBI/2025-26/79). Minimum 2FA with one dynamic factor; Sec. 8 lets issuers apply a **risk-based approach** using behavioural/contextual signals and "additional checks beyond the minimum two-factor authentication", and to "explore using DigiLocker as a platform for notification and confirmation for high-risk transactions". [R][P] — [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12898)

**RBI (Commercial Banks – Digital Payment Security Controls) Directions, 2026: IN FORCE 31 Jul 2026**
- RBI/DoS/2026-27/411; effective on issue; repeals earlier digital payment security instructions for commercial banks. Para 53 requires banks to parameterise alerts on transaction velocity, **"addition of new beneficiaries"**, and transactions to payees **"on whom vishing fraud"** is recorded, plus device/IP/geo signals; para 46 alerts for all transactions and new beneficiaries; para 64 caution customers on "phishing, vishing, reverse phishing"; para 66 a way to "identify / mark a transaction as fraudulent" with immediate notification and work toward instant reporting to the beneficiary entity. **No APP lag, cooling period or kill switch** in the text. [S: summary of the directions] — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-issues-commercial-banks-digital-payment-security-controls-directions-2026.html)

**Other RBI items (draft/in progress)**
- Draft revision of customer-liability rules (6 Mar 2026): widen limited-liability coverage, shorter complaint timelines, compensation for small-value frauds. [R] — [RBI Annual Report 2025-26](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/AnnualReportPublications.aspx?Id=1466)
- Draft KYC Amendment Directions (mid-Sep 2026): temporary **debit hold up to 60 days** on suspected mule transactions (₹1,000+) or accounts; customer notified, 20 days to explain. [R][S] — [Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/wealth/save/rbi-proposes-60-day-temporary-debit-hold-on-suspicious-money-mule-transactions-key-things-you-need-to-know-about-rbis-kyc-directions/articleshow/134241278.cms)
- In the Supreme Court's suo motu "digital arrest" case (order 9 Feb 2026), RBI said a **"liability framework to address compensation" for fraud-induced authorized transactions** is under consideration, plus a draft SOP for temporary debit holds and staff accountability for KYC lapses; MuleHunter.ai adopted by 26 banks. [S] — [LiveLaw, 10 Feb 2026](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/top-stories/supreme-court-digital-arrest-scam-suo-motu-mha-status-report-sop-rbi-cbi-dot-meity-victim-compensation-liability-framework-522549)
- MHA Inter-Departmental Committee (constituted 26 Dec 2025): "accountability of such entities must be ensured" where victims' losses stem from negligence or service deficiency by banks, telcos or other regulated entities; victims should not bear losses from "systemic failures or regulatory non-compliance"; RBI has issued an advisory to banks on AI-based fraud tools. [S] — [LiveLaw, 15 Jan 2026](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/top-stories/supreme-court-digital-arrests-victim-compensation-inter-departmental-committee-mha-liability-of-banks-telecoms-victim-loss-negligence-fraud-519132)
- Supreme Court (reported 10 Aug 2026): RBI to issue an SOP within four weeks on temporary debit holds for mule accounts; States to implement e-Zero FIR and Cyber Crime Coordination Centres. Digital-arrest complaints on NCRP: **1,23,672 (2024), 58,249 (2025), 16,377 (to 30 Jun 2026)**; Money Restoration Mechanism restored ₹18.05 crore in 36,290 cases. [S] — [Drishti IAS](https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/supreme-court-directs-measures-to-curb-digital-arrest-scams/print_manually)
- Digital-arrest losses: ₹1,935.51 crore in 2024 (~1.23 lakh complaints), ₹339 crore in 2023, ₹91.14 crore in 2022 (Rajya Sabha reply). [S] — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=504907)

### Inferences
- Pehchaan does **not** depend on the APP discussion paper becoming law: Sec. 8 of the Authentication Directions (risk-based extra checks, DigiLocker confirmation) and para 53 of the 2026 Security Controls Directions (new-beneficiary and vishing-payee monitoring) already let a bank add a "Who asked you to pay?" step and a hold today. The discussion paper is upside, not a prerequisite.
- The paper's lag is blunt (every APP at or above ₹10k for eligible accounts). Because ~45% of fraud *cases* sit above ₹10k but so do a large share of genuine payments, a selective trigger (call context + new payee + risk signals) is the natural "decision engine" pitch; the paper itself leaves whitelisting open (Q6), which is where Pehchaan's GO path fits.
- The court/MHA track (liability for negligence, compensation for fraud-induced authorised payments) raises the value of a **signed, timestamped evidence pack**: if liability shifts toward banks, banks need proof of what warning/verification was offered.

### Gaps
- No RBI draft or final directions on the 1-hour lag / trusted person found as of 24 Oct 2026; I could not confirm whether RBI has published a summary of comments. Check RBI press releases before the pitch.
- Final status of the March 2026 customer-liability draft is unconfirmed (one result hinted at amendment directions dated 21 Oct 2026, but the page did not load).
- The Supreme Court order date behind the Aug 2026 directions was not given in the source.

## 2. India: NPCI/UPI rail features

### Takeaway
NPCI has removed the "collect request" pull scam and forced verified beneficiary names, and UPI Circle already has a "secondary initiates, primary approves with PIN" flow. I found **no NPCI cooling-off/"pause" feature and no public NPCI fraud-risk score exposed to apps**, so call-time intervention is left to apps/banks.

### Cited Findings
| Control | Who / when | What it does | Outcome data | Pehchaan fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Verified beneficiary name display | NPCI; from 30 Jun 2025 | UPI apps must show the name from the bank's core banking system (not QR/nickname names) and disable editing of beneficiary names — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/banking/upi-to-display-verified-names-of-recipients-from-june-30-2025), [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2025/06/223-npci-upi-changes-transaction-speed-fraud-prevention/) [S]. One source gives 1 Jun 2026 and P2P+P2M scope — [Sansa Legal](https://www.sansalegal.com/post/upi-verified-name-display-rule-what-changed-for-digital-payments-from-june-1) [S] (conflict, unresolved) | None published found | Complement: the name is correct, but in voice-clone scams the victim knowingly pays a "new account" the caller names; CoP-style checks do not answer "who asked you" |
| End of P2P collect requests | NPCI circular 29 Jul 2025; effective 1 Oct 2025 | Banks/apps can no longer initiate or process P2P collect; merchant collect continues; earlier P2P collect cap ₹2,000 — [The Week/PTI](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/08/14/dcm65-biz-npci-collect.html) [S] | None published found | Complement: all P2P is now payer-initiated push, i.e. exactly the APP flow Pehchaan guards |

| Digital-arrest advisory | NPCI | Public warning to PhonePe/GPay/Paytm users on digital-arrest scams — [Digit](https://www.digit.in/news/general/phonepe-google-pay-paytm-users-beware-npci-issues-warning-over-rising-digital-arrest-scam-in-india.html) [S] | n/a | Complement (awareness only) |

### Inferences
- NPCI's controls act on the **payee/request** (name, collect), not on the **payer's situation** (being on a call, being coached). That leaves the "during or just after a call" moment uncovered at rail level.
- UPI Circle shows NPCI already accepts a two-person approval flow on UPI; a Pehchaan Family Verify could be framed as "UPI Circle in reverse" (the elder initiates, the trusted person confirms), consistent with RBI's Option 2.

### Gaps
- No primary NPCI circulars were opened in this pass (npci.org.in not reached); dates are from press.
- I found no public evidence of an NPCI real-time **fraud risk score shared with apps**, or any NPCI "pause/cooling" feature. The RBI/RBIH DPIP Phase-2 transaction risk score is in pilot with 7 banks [R] — [RBI AR 2025-26](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/AnnualReportPublications.aspx?Id=1466).

## 3. India: telecom and government fraud infrastructure (DoT, TRAI, I4C, RBIH)

### Takeaway
India already has national-scale **number reputation (FRI on DIP)**, **mule/suspect sharing (I4C Suspect Registry, MuleHunter.ai)**, **post-fraud recovery (1930/CFCFRMS)** and, from 1 Nov 2026, **network caller-name display (CNAP)**; banks must call from **1600-series** numbers. None of these verifies the *claim* a caller makes ("I'm your son"), but several are inputs Pehchaan should consume.

### Cited Findings
| Control | Who / when | What it does | Measured outcomes | Pehchaan fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Financial Fraud Risk Indicator (FRI) on Digital Intelligence Platform (DIP) | DoT; launched 21 May 2025 — [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2130249) [R][P] | Rates a mobile number as Medium/High/Very High risk; PhonePe declines Very High and is adding warnings for Medium; PhonePe, Paytm, GPay (90%+ of UPI) integrating — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/dot-launches-financial-fraud-risk-indicator-to-aid-cybercrime-detection-125052101912_1.html) [S] | ₹5,043.73 crore of suspected fraud blocked by Aug 2026; 1,600+ organisations on DIP — [ET Government](https://government.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/digital-india/5043-crore-saved-how-india-is-building-a-real-time-defence-against-digital-fraud/133989749) [R][S] | Dependency/input: FRI of the payee's linked number and of the caller's number are strong PAUSE/HOLD features |

CFCFRMS cumulative ₹11,158 crore "saved" in 32.80 lakh complaints to 30 Jun 2026 [R] | Complement: Pehchaan's signed evidence pack targets the speed/completeness gap between "liened" and "refunded" |

### Inferences
- The national stack is strong on **"is this number/account bad?"** and weak on **"is this person who they say they are, right now?"** A cloned-voice call from a fresh mule SIM claiming to be a relative has a clean FRI, an unknown CNAP name and no 1600 prefix: only an out-of-band check with the real relative resolves it.
- 1600 + CNAP make the **bank-impersonation** variant cheap to verify (Pehchaan's registry check); the **family-impersonation** variant still needs Family Verify / safe word.

### Gaps
- FRI and Chakshu outcome figures are carried from the repo's earlier research; not re-opened in this pass.
- No public data on 1600-series compliance after the 2026 deadlines.
- No public API for "is bank X calling customer Y now?" exists in India that I found; this is a Pehchaan assumption to state as a dependency.

## 4. India: what payment apps, banks, telcos and Truecaller do against call-led scams

### Takeaway
The closest existing product to Pehchaan's trigger is **PhonePe's Device Guard**, which already warns when a user "appears to be on a phone call" and behaves unusually, and **Google's in-call screen-share warning** piloted with Google Pay, Paytm and Navi. Both **warn**; neither verifies who asked for the payment. I found no comparable public feature from **Amazon Pay** (the sponsor) or from SBI/HDFC/ICICI beyond beneficiary cooling, limits and awareness.

### Cited Findings
- **PhonePe** (blog, 27 Mar 2025): "Device Guard" anti-abuse models target social-engineering; if a user appears to be on a phone call and shows unusual behaviour it warns them and asks whether to proceed or stop; PhonePe Protect lets users confirm declined transactions; identity graph links numbers, user IDs, accounts and VPAs. No figures given. [P] — [PhonePe](https://www.phonepe.com/blog/trust-and-safety/phonepes-guardrails-advanced-risk-detection-features/). PhonePe declines transactions linked to "Very High" FRI numbers. [S] — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/dot-launches-financial-fraud-risk-indicator-to-aid-cybercrime-detection-125052101912_1.html). **Fit: Overlap** on the trigger (on-call detection); Pehchaan's difference is the verification step and the GO/PAUSE/HOLD outcome.
- **Google Pay / Google in India** (20 Nov 2025): pilot with **Navi, Paytm and Google Pay** that warns when a user opens the app while screen-sharing on a call with an unknown number (Android 11+), with one-tap end call; Google Pay surfaces **"more than a million warnings each week"** for potentially fraudulent transactions; on-device Scam Detection (Gemini Nano) for calls, Hindi and English, **Pixel 9+ only** (Pixel share in India under 1%); Play Protect blocked 115 million risky sideload attempts in India in 2025. [S] — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/20/google-steps-up-ai-scam-protection-in-india-but-gaps-remain/). **Fit: Overlap** (screen-share-on-call warning) / Complement (Pehchaan also covers calls without screen sharing, e.g. a cloned relative asking for money).
- **Amazon Pay**: reported adding biometric (fingerprint/face) UPI authentication, device-bound [S] — [PaymentsJournal](https://www.paymentsjournal.com/amazon-pay-is-one-of-the-first-wallets-to-support-biometrics-on-upi); an older interview describes a risk engine that can step up with OTP, send to manual review or reverse [S, undated] — [Gizbot](https://www.gizbot.com/amphtml/internet/news/amazon-pay-tech-features-interview-080986.html). **No public in-call or APP-specific scam control found.** Fit: **white space** for the sponsor.
- **SBI**: new internet-banking beneficiaries activate after 1 hour (4 hours for some categories), and transfers to a new beneficiary are capped at **₹5 lakh aggregate for the first 4 days**; up to 4 beneficiaries per bank type per day. [P] — [SBI FAQ](https://onlinesbi.sbi.bank.in/sbijava/new_ben_add_faq_0.html). SBI YONO blog (Sep 2025) lists fraud monitoring, device binding, limits, instant alerts. [P] — [SBI](https://sbi.bank.in/web/yono/blog/digital-arrest-scams-protect-yourself-from-cybercriminals). Fit: Complement (blunt cooling on new beneficiaries; does not apply to UPI VPA pay-to-new-payee in the same way).
- **HDFC Bank**: awareness ("Vigil Aunty" campaign), advice to only take bank calls from 1600 numbers, enable 2FA. [P] — [HDFC Bank](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/digital-arrest). **ICICI**: nothing specific found.
- **Airtel**: network-level AI spam detection since Sep 2024; **98.4 billion suspected spam calls and 4.4 billion spam SMS flagged in two years** [S] — [FoneArena](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/?p=493419); Airtel says I4C analysis (Sep 2024–Jun 2025) showed **68.7% lower financial losses** and 14.3% fewer cybercrime incidents on its network [S, company-reported] — [MobiGyaan](https://www.mobigyaan.com/airtels-anti-fraud-customer-losses-70). **Jio**: built-in network spam-call alerts reported rolling out (date unverified) [S] — [The Tech Outlook](https://www.thetechoutlook.com/telecom/jio-new-spam-call-alerts-now-rolling-out-in-india-as-a-built-in-feature-for-incoming-calls/). **Vi**: "Vi Protect" unveiled at IMC 2025 [S] — [TeckNexus](https://tecknexus.com/vodafone-idea-protect-ai-powered-network-security-debuts-at-imc-2025/). Fit: Complement (they label the call; Pehchaan acts at the payment).
- **Truecaller**: AI Call Scanner (May 2024) merges the call to a Truecaller line, samples the caller's voice and returns "Human Detected"/"AI Voice Detected"; Premium, Android, US first; in June 2024 it worked only with English audio, Hindi in progress; no accuracy figures published. [S] — [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2024/05/223-truecaller-ai-voice-scanner-spam-call/), [MediaNama interview](https://www.medianama.com/2024/06/223-video-interview-truecaller-ai-voice-scanner/). Scam Checker launched in India 28 Sep 2026 (check a number/link/message, describe a situation). [R][S] — [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-truecaller-scam-checker-apac-india/). Fit: Overlap with Pehchaan's shadow-mode voice detector; Pehchaan should not compete on voice detection.

### Inferences
- The "during a call" signal is no longer novel in India (PhonePe, Google). Pehchaan's defensible novelty is (a) asking **who** asked and (b) **checking that answer out of band**, then (c) a graded GO/PAUSE/HOLD with human release, and (d) the evidence pack.
- For the Amazon jury, the honest framing is: competitors already warn on calls; Amazon Pay has no public equivalent; Pehchaan gives Amazon Pay a verification-grade control that leapfrogs "warn and continue".

### Gaps
- No outcome data for PhonePe Device Guard or the Google India pilot.
- Jio's feature launch date and Truecaller AI Call Scanner India/Hindi availability could not be confirmed.
- ICICI and Paytm-specific in-app call-scam features not found.

## 5. Global regulatory controls (UK, Singapore, Australia, EU, US)

### Takeaway
The UK shifted APP losses to banks (mandatory reimbursement, 7 Oct 2024) and gave them a legal **hold of up to four business days**; Singapore uses **24-hour holds, 12-hour cooling on high-risk changes, Money Lock, kill switch and police Restriction Orders**; Australia has **CoP since July 2025** and a statutory **Scams Prevention Framework** in full effect from 31 Mar 2027; the EU made **Verification of Payee** mandatory on 9 Oct 2025 and agreed bank-impersonation refunds (PSR, not yet in force); the US treats AI-voice robocalls as "artificial voice" under the TCPA. Results are mostly self-reported; reimbursement data is the most robust.

### Inferences
- Every major jurisdiction converged on **friction at the payer's side for risky first-time/large transfers** (UK 4-day hold, Singapore 24h hold and 12h cooling, RBI's proposed 1h lag). Pehchaan's 30-minute HOLD is shorter than all of them; it should be pitched as the *selective, verified* tier that can escalate to the bank's statutory hold, not as a replacement.
- Name-check (CoP/VoP, India's verified name display) is now table stakes; it does not stop scams where the victim is told to pay a "new" account. Caller-verification (Monzo, CBA) and question flows (Westpac, Revolut) are the true analogues of Pehchaan, and the FOS evidence that coached victims lie to questionnaires is the strongest argument for **out-of-band verification instead of self-report**.
- Reimbursement regimes (UK) and liability debates (EU PSR, India's SC/MHA track) create the commercial reason a bank pays for Pehchaan.

### Gaps
- No independent evaluation of CoP/VoP, Money Lock, SRF or SaferPay effectiveness found; figures are bank/industry self-reports.
- UK delay-power commencement date conflict (FCA page says 30 Oct 2024; not reconciled).
- Australian "call-during-payment" mandates (beyond CallerCheck/SaferPay) not found; draft SPF codes not read in full.
- Singapore SRF payout/claim figures not published in the sources found.

## 6. Platform-level protections (Android, Apple, Google Pay)

### Takeaway
Android now blocks the classic remote-control playbook **during calls with non-contacts** (sideloading, accessibility grants, disabling Play Protect on Android 16) and warns with a **30-second pause** when a participating banking app is opened while screen-sharing with an unknown caller (UK, US, India, Brazil pilots). Apple offers call screening of unknown numbers but no banking/in-call API I could find. These protect against device takeover and screen-share scams, **not** against a voice-cloned "relative" persuading a user to pay on their own.

### Cited Findings
- Android (13 May 2025): during a call with a non-contact, Android blocks disabling Play Protect (Android 6+), first-time sideloading and granting accessibility permissions (Android 16), and prompts to stop screen sharing when the call ends; banking-app screen-share warning on Android 11+, UK pilot with **Monzo, NatWest, Revolut**; Scam Detection in Messages/Phone expanded to financial impersonation; Key Verifier for contacts. No measured figures. [P] — [Google blog](https://blog.google/security/whats-new-in-android-security-privacy-2025/)
- Android (3 Dec 2025): in-call protection for financial apps expanded to "most major UK banks", pilots in **Brazil and India**, US pilot from Dec 2025 with Cash App, JPMorganChase and P2P apps; warning has a **"Wait 30 seconds to continue"** pause and one-tap end call/stop sharing; UK pilot "has already helped thousands of users end calls" (no rate given). [P] — [Google blog](https://blog.google/security/android-expands-pilot-in-call-scam-protection-financial-apps/)
- India specifics (Navi, Paytm, Google Pay; Pixel-only Scam Detection; 1M+ GPay warnings/week): see Section 4. [S] — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/20/google-steps-up-ai-scam-protection-in-india-but-gaps-remain/)
- Apple (10 Oct 2025): iOS 26 Call Screening (unknown callers asked for name and reason before ringing), unknown-sender message filtering, spam link disabling; no banking/in-call features or figures. [P] — [Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/sg/newsroom/2025/10/apple-helps-safeguard-users-from-scams-and-fraud-while-protecting-personal-data/). Call Screening is manual, not AI spam scoring, and only for numbers not in contacts. [S] — [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2025/06/13/security-bite-apples-new-ios-26-spam-tools-will-make-scammers-cry/)

### Inferences
- The Android 30-second pause is the closest platform analogue to Pehchaan's PAUSE, and Google (a big platform) chose a **short, unconditional pause** without verification. Pehchaan's PAUSE adds the verification that makes the pause useful.
- **Platform dependency**: Pehchaan's "during or just after a call" trigger needs call-state access inside the UPI app. Android exposes call state (with permission); iOS is far more restrictive, so an iOS build may need user self-report or bank-side signals. State this as a known limitation.
- Platform features skip the cases Pehchaan targets: callers saved in contacts (a real relative's number spoofed or a "new number" story), and calls with no screen sharing.

### Gaps
- No outcome rates (warnings shown vs calls ended vs losses avoided) published for Android in-call protections.
- Could not confirm whether the India in-call pilot has expanded beyond Navi/Paytm/GPay or added Indian-language warnings.

## 7. How often does fraud happen during an active call, and does cooling-off/friction work?

### Takeaway
The best quantitative signal is BioCatch's: **more than 1 in 4 confirmed fraud cases had the victim on an active call, an active call is 30 times more prevalent in fraud than genuine sessions, and fewer than 1% of Android users combine calls with mobile banking** — strong support for a call-context trigger with a low genuine-user hit rate. Evidence that delays alone stop scams is **thin and mostly self-reported**; the experimental evidence favours short pauses, but coached victims defeat self-report questions.

### Cited Findings
**Call-related prevalence**
- BioCatch (BAI paper, Jan 2022): "Fewer than 1% of all Android users combine phone calls with mobile banking activity"; "More than one in four confirmed cases of fraud show that the victim was on an active phone call"; "an active call is 30 times more prevalent in the fraud population than the genuine population". [P] — [BioCatch/BAI PDF](https://www.biocatch.com/hubfs/BAI-Protecting-Customers-Jan-2022.pdf) (verified in the PDF text)
- BioCatch EMEA 2023: over 70% of remote-access-trojan scams originated via a phone call [S, via search summary] — [BioCatch](https://www.biocatch.com/press-release/emea-fraud-intelligence-annual-report-2023). BioCatch Global Scams report (30 Sep 2026): attempted banking scams +35%, 9 in 10 scam sessions on mobile; uses active-call-during-session as a detection indicator but published press material gives no share. [S] — [BioCatch](https://www.biocatch.com/press-release/global-banking-scams-increase-by-35)
- UK 2025: 17% of APP cases begin via telecoms but carry 28% of losses. [P] — [UK Finance](https://www.ukfinance.org.uk/news-and-insight/press-release/fraud-report-2026-press-release)
- Singapore H1 2025: 79% of scam cases were authorised/self-effected transfers. [P] — [MAS](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/parliamentary-replies/2026/written-reply-to-pqs-on-srf-and-fraud-surveillance)
- Australia 2024: phone calls were the highest-loss contact method (A$107.2m on Scamwatch). [P] — [NASC](https://www.nasc.gov.au/node/130)
- India: RBI's discussion paper cites no call-pressure figure [P] — [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/PublicationsView.aspx?id=23810); digital-arrest (call/video-call coercion) complaints 1,23,672 in 2024 [S] — [Drishti IAS](https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/supreme-court-directs-measures-to-curb-digital-arrest-scams/print_manually).

**Friction / cooling-off effectiveness**
- Experimental: a brief "incubation" period (delay or distraction) reduces consumers' engagement with fraudulent offers and lowers perceived legitimacy, across multiple studies (Journal of Consumer Policy, 2026). [P] — [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10603-026-09619-3)
- Counter-evidence: in sales contexts, people offered cooling-off periods almost never cancel (Deakin University study). [S] — [The New Daily](https://www.thenewdaily.com.au/finance/your-budget/2016/11/28/cooling-off-period)
- Self-reported field outcomes: Google's 30-second in-call pause helped "thousands of users end calls" (no rate) [P] — [Google](https://blog.google/security/android-expands-pilot-in-call-scam-protection-financial-apps/); Australian CoP: one bank saw 450,000+ payments abandoned after "no match" [P] — [ABA](https://www.ausbanking.org.au/banks-hit-major-milestones-as-scam-fighting-technology-stops-thousands-of-risky-transfers/); Westpac SaferPay >A$1m averted [P] — [Westpac](https://www.westpac.com.au/about-westpac/media/media-releases/2024/13-June/); Airtel network alerts: 68.7% lower losses (company-reported, I4C-analysed) [S] — [MobiGyaan](https://www.mobigyaan.com/airtels-anti-fraud-customer-losses-70).
- Moral hazard: PSR found "no indication of people being significantly less cautious" after full reimbursement. [P] — [PSR](https://www.psr.org.uk/information-for-consumers/app-scams-reimbursement-dashboard/)
- Limits of self-report friction: FOS decisions show scammer-coached customers choose false payment purposes, defeating warnings. [P] — [FOS DRN-5249672](https://www.financial-ombudsman.org.uk/decision/DRN-5249672.pdf)
- Limits of long holds for groomed victims: Singapore Restriction Orders — 2 of 12 ran to the maximum five extensions; only 1 lifted as "no longer at risk". [P] — [MHA Singapore](https://www.mha.gov.sg/media-room/newsroom/restriction-orders-issued-under-protection-from-scams-act-2025/)
- Public preference (US, ABA survey 2023): delay/cooling-off ranked below better detection and more warnings. [S] — [ABA Banking Journal](https://bankingjournal.aba.com/2023/04/americans-blame-themselves-for-falling-for-real-time-payment-scams/)

### Inferences
- The BioCatch ratios justify Pehchaan's trigger design: active-call + payment is rare among genuine users (<1% of Android users) yet present in >25% of confirmed fraud. Pehchaan's own Pay-to-hold numbers should be presented next to this, with the 2022/Android caveat.
- A pure timer (RBI's 1-hour lag, Google's 30 seconds) relies on the victim reflecting; coached and deeply groomed victims often do not. Coupling the pause with a **third party** (relative's phone, bank registry) is what the evidence suggests is missing.

### Gaps
- No Indian figure for the share of UPI/bank fraud executed **during** an active call. No public A/B or outcome study of cooling-off periods for APP fraud found (UK 4-day hold usage, Singapore 24h hold outcomes, DBS 12h cooling all unpublished).
- BioCatch's 1-in-4 figure is from 2022 and from BioCatch customers (mostly non-India).

## 8. How to position Pehchaan against this landscape

### Takeaway
Position Pehchaan as **the verification-and-decision layer at the payer's moment of risk**: it consumes India's national signals (FRI, Suspect Registry, MuleHunter, 1600/CNAP), applies RBI-style friction selectively instead of to every ₹10k payment, and adds the step nobody in India ships: **checking "who asked you to pay" with the real person, out of band**.

### Cited Findings
- Incumbent overlap to acknowledge on stage: PhonePe Device Guard warns on-call users [P] — [PhonePe](https://www.phonepe.com/blog/trust-and-safety/phonepes-guardrails-advanced-risk-detection-features/); Google's 30-second in-call pause with GPay/Paytm/Navi [S] — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/20/google-steps-up-ai-scam-protection-in-india-but-gaps-remain/); question flows like Westpac SaferPay [P] — [Westpac](https://www.westpac.com.au/about-westpac/media/media-releases/2024/13-June/); caller verification like Monzo Call Status and CBA CallerCheck [P] — [Android Developers Blog](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2024/03/battling-impersonation-scams-monzo-innovative-approach.html), [CommBank](https://www.commbank.com.au/articles/newsroom/2023/02/namecheck-callercheck-scam-detection.html).
- Regulatory hooks already in force: Authentication Directions Sec. 8 [P] — [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12898); Digital Payment Security Controls Directions para 53/66 [S] — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-issues-commercial-banks-digital-payment-security-controls-directions-2026.html); proposed: RBI APP paper [P] — [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/PublicationsView.aspx?id=23810).

### Inferences (positioning bullets)
1. **"Decision engine, not another blocklist."** Say up front that number reputation (FRI), mule scoring (MuleHunter), suspect sharing (Suspect Registry), name display and CNAP already exist; Pehchaan *consumes* them as inputs and adds the missing check of the claim behind the payment. This pre-empts the "isn't this just FRI?" question.
2. **Deployable under rules in force today; the RBI paper is upside.** Sec. 8 of the Authentication Directions (risk-based extra checks, DigiLocker confirmation) and para 53 of the July 2026 Security Controls Directions (new-beneficiary and vishing-payee monitoring) already let a bank run GO/PAUSE/HOLD. Say clearly that the 1-hour lag and trusted-person rule are **proposed (9 Apr 2026), not final**.
3. **Selective friction instead of blanket friction.** RBI's lag hits every eligible APP at or above ₹10k; Pehchaan applies PAUSE/HOLD only when call context, a new payee and risk signals coincide, which BioCatch's data supports (<1% of genuine Android users bank while on a call vs >25% of fraud cases). Pitch the GO path as RBI's open whitelisting question (Q6) answered with data.
4. **Verification beats self-report.** UK ombudsman cases show coached victims answer "family/friend" to payment-purpose questions. Pehchaan's answer gets **checked**: the relative confirms on their own phone, the bank confirms via a 1600-number call registry (India's version of Monzo Call Status / CBA CallerCheck, made feasible by TRAI's 1600 mandate and CNAP from 1 Nov 2026), or the safe word matches. Name the bank-side registry API as a dependency.
5. **Family Verify = RBI's "trusted person", scoped to the risky moment.** Mirror the paper's guardrails (consent, 24-hour cooling to change the trusted person, opt-out after 24 hours) and point to UPI Circle's partial-delegation PIN approval as proof the rails already support two-person approval.
6. **Shortest hold, with escalation.** Pehchaan's 30-minute HOLD with human release is lighter than the UK (up to 4 business days), Singapore (24-hour holds, 12-hour cooling) and RBI's proposed 1 hour; present it as the first tier that hands off to the bank's longer statutory hold or the draft 60-day mule debit hold when verification fails.
7. **Evidence pack aimed at the "liened vs refunded" gap and the liability debate.** FY24–FY26: ₹56,087 crore reported, ₹9,079 crore liened, ₹206 crore refunded (PIB). RBI told the Supreme Court a liability framework for fraud-induced authorised payments is under consideration, so a signed record of what was asked, verified and warned has value to both victim and bank. Claim faster, more complete reports, not higher recovery.
8. **Voice detection stays a clue (G2), in line with the market.** Truecaller's AI Call Scanner and Google's Pixel-only Scam Detection show voice/AI-call detection is narrow (English-first, <1% device reach in India) and nobody holds money on it. For Amazon Pay, which has no public in-call or APP-specific control, Pehchaan is a step past PhonePe/Google's "warn and continue".

### Gaps
- Whether Amazon Pay has unpublicised in-call controls is unknown; avoid saying "Amazon Pay has nothing", say "no public feature found".
- Pehchaan's own Pay-to-hold and false-positive numbers must come from `ml/realcall/FINDINGS.md` / `engine/results/deck_numbers.json`, not from this landscape note.

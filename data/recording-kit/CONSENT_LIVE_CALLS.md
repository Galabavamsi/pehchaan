# Consent to record a phone call for research (Pehchaan, RAKSHAM hackathon)

**Team:** humanslop (Galaba Vamsi, Varun Rai), IIT Bhilai. **Contact:** galabavamsi12@gmail.com

## What we're asking

You make one or more ordinary phone or WhatsApp calls to our test phone and talk normally for about a
minute (any topic, any language). We record what arrives on the test phone.

## Why

We are testing whether software that detects AI-cloned voices wrongly flags *real* people on real phone
lines. Your genuine voice helps us measure and reduce those false alarms.

## What we will and won't do

- **We will:** record the call audio on our test phone and use it only to test and train our detector
  for the RAKSHAM hackathon (IIT Delhi, finale 24–25 October 2026).
- **We will never:** clone or imitate your voice, publish or share your recording, or use it for anything else.
- Your name is replaced by a code (for example V03). Your phone number is not stored.
- Recordings are kept in private, encrypted storage and **deleted by 31 December 2026**.
- You can withdraw at any time, even after the call. Message us and we delete your recordings within 7 days.

## Not runnable in software (declared)

| Evasion | Status today |
|---|---|
| Pay from another UPI app that doesn't have Pehchaan | Not covered by the app SDK. At a bank deployment the risk API sees every debit from the account, but without the call state, so only payee and amount context. |
| Screen-share or remote-control app (the scammer taps for the victim) | Not detected today. Android can tell an app that screen sharing or an accessibility service is active: we would add that as a context signal. |
| The scammer coaches the real son, or the son is in on it | Family Verify trusts the bound relative. The son sees the payee name and amount; the case stays in the analyst queue; large amounts need an analyst to release a hold. |
| The scammer adds their own phone to the family circle before the attack | Both sides opt in. Production binding goes through the bank app's authenticated device binding with a waiting period for new relatives; the prototype binds the relative page when it opens. |

RAKSHAM · AI Cybersecurity Hackathon Track 01 — Voice Cloning Fraud Detection
Pehchaanपहचान
Verify the person, not the voice.

A safeguard inside UPI and bank apps. When someone pays a new or unusual payee during a call, Pehchaan asks "who asked you to pay?" and checks that claim through channels a cloned voice cannot reach: the relative's own registered phone, the bank's 1600-number call registry, a family safe word. Our Indian-language clone detector adds evidence, but never decides alone.

On a call + new payee + ≥₹10k. Metadata only, no audio.

2Ask

"Who asked you to pay?" Family · Bank · Police · Other

3Verify

A check a clone can't pass, then GO PAUSE HOLD

Three patterns, one ending

T1 Cloned relative from an unknown, overseas or WhatsApp number

T2 Fake bank official, often an AI voice agent working at scale

T3 Digital arrest: fake police plus a cloned relative on the same call

All three end in an authorised push payment, made with the victim's own credentials.

Our bounded problem

One moment: a payment to a new or unusual payee made during, or ≤10 min after, a call (≤60 min from ₹50,000). Every other payment is left untouched.

Our test set: 7,809 genuine / 11,265 cloned clips, 13 generators (6 never seen in training), 5 phone channels, one global threshold. Open-source detectors lose ~48% AUC on real 2024 deepfakes7; real-time voice conversion adds only ~0.4 s19, so clones hold live conversations.

Insight

A clone can copy a voice. It cannot answer from your son's phone, call from your bank's 1600 number, or know your family's safe word.

So Pehchaan verifies the identity claim, and treats voice forensics as one capped signal.

Each claim goes to the check that defeats it: family → the relative's own device, bank → the bank's live 1600 call registry, police → "there is no digital arrest". The clone detector is one capped signal.

{{icon:target}}Targets RBI's proposed controls

RBI proposes a 1-hour lag on transfers above ₹10k and trusted-person approval above ₹50k for people 70+6. Pehchaan applies them only where risk is real: in our simulation just 0.21% of genuine triggered payments are held, while 73% of scams are.

{{icon:waveform}}Indian-language, phone-line forensics

Fine-tuned on consented clones of our own voices in Telugu, Hindi and English: 4.8% EER vs 34% off-the-shelf, 6.0% on generators it never saw. When a real call fooled it, we put it in shadow mode instead of letting it judge.

05 · Detection & decision logic explainable fusion + hard guardrails

Six independent signal families, fused as explainable log-odds, with guardrails so no single weak signal can stop anyone.

Signal families · cap on each family's pull
C
Claim · cap 60×
User's one-tap answer: family / bank / police-govt / other / no one
K
Call context · cap 30×
Caller not in contacts (on-device hash), international, VoIP, bank claim from a non-1600 number, DoT FRI tier, call >15 min
P

New payee, amount above the user's p95/p99, payee flagged (suspect registry / mule score), payee name ≠ claimed person, burst of new payees

Each bar is ln(likelihood ratio) after family caps, exactly as the analyst console shows it. Measured voice ratios: 57–70× on simulated channels, but 0.93× (no information) on a real call, hence shadow mode. The others are documented assumptions, recalibrated in shadow mode.

Tiers

GO p < 15% · PAUSE 15–75% · HOLD ≥ 75% and G1, or G7

Thresholds tuned to an alert budget: ≤0.25% of genuine triggered payments held, ≤6% paused.

Guardrails (hard rules)
G1

No single weak signal. HOLD needs ≥2 families each ≥3×, not counting what fired the trigger (new payee, amount) or voice.

G2

Voice only raises suspicion, never creates a HOLD, never reassures. Today it runs in shadow mode until it passes a real-call gate.

G3

Quality gate. <4 s speech or SNR <10 dB → inconclusive, never "real".

G4

Decisive yes. Relative's phone, or the bank's 1600 registry, confirms → GO.

G5

Trusted payees (paid ≥3×, usual amount) are never interrupted.

G6

No accusation. Copy says what we noticed and what to check, in Hindi or English.

G7

Decisive no. Relative's signed "not me" or the bank's "we're not calling" → HOLD, always. Self-reported checks stay advisory.

07 · Privacy, security, safety & misuse safeguards

No call recording by default, no accusations, no automatic blocking, and a human for every high-impact decision.

{{icon:eye-slash}}What data we use, and what we never touch
InputWhyConsent · retention
Call state, caller numberTrigger, contextApp permission · number hashed at rest, 90 days
Contacts"Known caller?"Matched on-device by hash; never uploaded
Payment fieldsPayment contextAlready held by the bank/PSP
8–12 s voice clipVoice checkOpt-in per call; discarded after scoring unless saved as evidence

Never collected: call recordings by default · SMS/WhatsApp content · location history · the user's voiceprint · contact lists.

{{icon:scales}}When we're wrong
  • False positive: PAUSE always allows "continue". HOLD is a delay, not a block: it releases on any positive check or an analyst callback, and is never auto-sent when it expires; the user re-confirms.
  • Appeal: "This wasn't a scam" in one tap → analyst label → recalibration. FP rates are reported per cohort.
  • False negative: one-tap report → signed evidence pack → 1930/NCRP pre-fill; payee VPA flagged to the PSP mule team.
  • Who can act: the user (continue / verify / cancel), the relative (yes / no only), the bank analyst (release / confirm fraud; RBAC, every access logged). No automated police reports.
{{icon:detective}}Fraudster adaptation → our counter
Coaches victim: "tap No one"Context still scores; screen says "if someone is telling you what to tap, stop"
Hang up, then pay10-minute post-call window
New voice generatorVoice capped; O-checks decisive; monthly red-team refresh; held-out eval
Codec laundering / noiseChannel augmentation in training; quality gate → inconclusive
Spoofed "Yes, it's me"Device-bound key + signed challenge; new devices wait 72 h before they count
{{icon:shield-check}}Misuse, security, fairness
  • Abuse of the family circle: both sides opt in; per-payment, payer-initiated; the relative sees amount band + payee name only, no transaction history, no monitoring.
  • Evidence access: KMS-encrypted, S3 Object Lock, hash chain; opening audio needs a case ID + reason, and is logged.
  • Fairness: detector false-reject rates published by language and gender; elderly mode (large text, read-aloud, vernacular).
  • LLM safety: the cue classifier returns structured labels only, via Bedrock Guardrails; it never writes user-facing verdicts.
Designed for the DPDP Act 2023 principles of consent, purpose limitation and data minimisation. Threat model and mitigations in detail: architecture page 3.08 / 12

10 · We attacked it ourselves adversarial robustness · scripted through the live API · 24 Oct

We ran 15 attacks through the live pipeline: 7 stopped, 6 paused and asked, 2 still get through. The 3 holes the run found are fixed.

Stopped before money moves · 7
  • Spoofed 1600 "bank" number → the bank's own call registry says it isn't calling → HOLD
  • Caller ID shows the son's saved number (spoofed, or his WhatsApp taken over) → his own phone says "not me" → HOLD
  • Mule account opened in the son's name → his phone decides → HOLD
  • Swap the attacker's phone in as the son's, mid-attack → refused
  • Forge or replay the son's "yes" → signature rejected
  • Spam Family Verify until he gives in → 4th push refused: "call him on his saved number"
  • A clone the detector misses → still HOLD: voice never releases money
Paused and asked · 6
  • ₹9,500 four times to one payee during the call → every payment asked
  • Hang up, pay ₹60,000 half an hour later → asked (60-minute window from ₹50,000)
  • Coached to tap "No one asked" or "a shop" → three quick questions first. A victim coached through all three still pays: the residual risk
  • WhatsApp call instead of a phone call → asked
  • Several new payees during one call → burst signal on each
Genuine check

Real son, detector false alarm → paid: the detector can't hold money either way.

Still gets through · 2

₹40,000 paid 30 minutes after the call (outside the 10-minute window). New payees with no call at all: by design, no call means no Pehchaan.

Not testable in software · 4

Paying from an app without Pehchaan · screen-share or remote-control apps · the real son coached or in on it · a scammer joining the family circle before the attack. Each has a stated next step.

Fixed on site · rules v0.3

Ask whenever the caller is a family-circle member's saved number · Family Verify even when the score is Green · "shop / UPI app" answers get the refund-script questions · no re-binding a phone while a question waits · split payments add up · longer window for large amounts.

Each run on a fresh demo with fictional people, mock context adapters and the mock voice service, through the same API the phones use: app/server/redteam.py → RED_TEAM.md (16 runs: 15 attacks + 1 genuine check). Rule changes: SPEC.md v0.3. The simulator figures (A4) are for the v0.2 rules.11 / 12

Notifications & escalation
  • User: in-app screen; a push if the app is in the background; SMS fallback for hold status.
  • Relative: one push per payment; 3-min timeout → callback / safe word.
  • Analyst: queue sorted by amount × p; callback to the user's registered number, never the caller's.
  • After confirmed fraud: evidence pack → user submits 1930 / NCRP; payee VPA → PSP mule team and I4C registry feed.
Evidence pack contents

Case ID · IST timestamps · masked payer · payee VPA + bank · amount · caller number + series · claim · all reasons with contributions · voice score + model version + quality · transcript snippet (only with consent) · SHA-256 event chain · Ed25519 signature.

Limitations we report, not hide
  • Round 1's one real call (a dialer recording) flagged 91–95% of genuine speech. On 73 speakerphone test calls since, the failure was missed clones; retraining on real calls fixed most of it (A3 4/5).
  • A new cloner still wins: clips from Sarvam's instant voice cloning were caught 0/12 times on real calls by v1 and A (its stock Bulbul voices, left, are easy).
  • New speaker is hardest: held-out S02 16.6% EER on simulated channels (S01 6.8%).
  • Only 2 speakers, one carrier, one seed; the live-caller gate is not yet run.
  • Some TTS emit digital silence between words, a shortcut risk; trimming is applied to real and fake alike.
Watermark check (Chatterbox / Perth)

Detected in 100% of clean, PSTN and Opus clips, 88% replay, but only 30% after AMR-NB; false hits 0–0.7% (6.7% on replay). A positive-only bonus signal, never proof of "real".

Appendix A3 · 4/6 real phone calls · method · retraining · held-out handset

Real phone calls: what we measured, what retraining fixed, and what still gets through.

Method
  • Tape calls: Vobiz → Jio → a team phone on speaker → laptop mic. Each tape mixes ~10 s genuine team lines with content-matched clones of the same lines.
  • Alignment: start/end chirps by matched filter, items by envelope correlation; an item counts only with ≥40% speech and SNR ≥8 dB.
  • Pre-registered splits: speaker S02, Sarvam clones and the whole Redmi (D2) are test-only, fixed before any of their data existed.
  • Learned speakerphone channel fitted on train calls only (112 items, 24 calls): clean clones pushed through it are caught 27% of the time vs 28% on real calls, so it reproduces the real failure for training.
  • Hands-free: adb answers and turns on speaker; the last 19 Redmi calls ran unattended, 189/190 items usable.

Appendix A3 · 5/6 consent · data handling for real calls and the live demo

Every voice we cloned or recorded belongs to a consenting team member, and the code refuses anyone else.

{{icon:signature}}Who consented, to what
  • Cloning (2 people): the two team members signed a written form: recordings, synthetic copies with open-source and commercial tools (Sarvam named), role-play scam lines, hackathon use only, no public release.
  • Recording only (volunteers): a separate form that promises no cloning. Volunteers stay blocked in code until their signed form is saved.
  • Withdrawal at any time; data deleted within 7 days of asking.
{{icon:lock-key}}Enforced in code, not by policy
  • The call tools refuse to dial, record or clone anyone without a saved consent file, and won't answer any caller but our own test number.
  • Phone numbers live only in one private file; call logs store a hash.
  • Webhooks and audio URLs work only for calls we placed, and only while they're live.
{{icon:phone-call}}The live attack call
  • Plays a pre-recorded clone of a consenting teammate to a team phone; the scam script is role-play.
  • Never recorded, scored or added to any dataset; the clip is deleted when the call ends.
  • Every screen uses fictional people, banks and UPI IDs (DemoPay, Bharat Demo Bank).
{{icon:trash}}Storage and deletion
  • Audio and consent files: the team laptop and one private, encrypted cloud bucket, auto-deleted on 31 Dec 2026.
  • Cloned voices saved at Sarvam are deleted after the finale.
  • No real person's voice from the internet, ever. No customer or bank data anywhere.

Appendix A5 fusion likelihood ratios · simulator assumptions

Every number the decision engine uses, and where it comes from.

Likelihood ratios (scam vs genuine, given the trigger fired) · engine config v0.2
{{include:fragments/a4_lrs.html}}

All values start as documented team assumptions, each with a one-line rationale in the config, and are recalibrated in shadow mode before any user sees a screen. Voice LRs are replaced by the detector's measured calibrated band rates and floored at 1× (never reassuring). Watermark hits are capped by the V family cap.

Prior, tiers, budgets
  • Prior 1 in 400 scams per triggered payment: deliberately cautious. The public base rate (NPCI volumes × NCRP call-driven scams) gives ≈1 in 1,300.
  • GO <15% · PAUSE 15–75% · HOLD ≥75% + G1, or G7
  • Budgets: ≤0.25% of genuine triggered payments held; ≤6% paused. Both met.
Simulator assumptions (team assumptions, all swept)
  • Scam mix: T1 cloned relative 20% · T2 fake bank 45% · T3 digital arrest 35%, informed by Chakshu category counts1
  • Genuine mix: merchant/delivery 38% · friend 30% · relative 15% · elderly on noisy lines 12% · genuine 1600 bank call 5%
  • Behaviour: half of payers have a family circle · voice check run by 30% · checklist by 60% · callback by 35% · bank registry covers 70% of banks · 15–25% of coached victims answer "no one"
  • Not modelled: learning effects, repeat victims, and adversaries probing thresholds. That is why shadow mode comes first.
